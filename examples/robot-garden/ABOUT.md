# Robot Garden

This fixture was written from scratch. It is not an export of a real conversation, an anonymized chat, or a reconstruction of real events. Every robot, message, date, and garden detail is invented.

Mossbot, Pebblebot, and Orbitbot are imaginary gardening robots. Compostbot appears only as the fictional author of a forwarded note. The reserved `example.org` address is an illustrative link; the parser never opens it. `seed-map.txt` is a small invented text attachment.

There are **6 ordinary messages**, **3 senders**, and **1 service record**. The service record is excluded from the parsed message count. The pages are intentionally named `messages.html`, `messages2.html`, and `messages10.html` to exercise numeric page ordering; this is a test fixture, not an assertion that omitted live export pages are complete.

The fixture covers continued messages across page boundaries, an embedded link, line breaks, indented code, a reply, a forwarded note, a media marker, Unicode, and a day boundary. Messages span April 7–8, 2031, in UTC.
